## Respectful Workplace
We are committed to a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation. This applies to all interactions with colleagues, clients, and partners, in person and online.

## Reporting Concerns
If you experience or witness harassment, discrimination, or any breach of this code, you can report it through any of these channels:
- Your manager or HR business partner.
- The confidential Ethics Hotline (phone and web, available 24/7, anonymous reporting supported). Details are on the HR portal under "Speak Up".
- The Internal Complaints Committee for complaints of sexual harassment.

All reports are treated confidentially and investigated promptly. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself a disciplinary offence.

## Conflicts of Interest
Disclose any outside employment, directorships, or financial interests that could conflict with your duties via the Conflict of Interest form on the HR portal.

## Gifts and Hospitality
Gifts from clients or vendors valued above USD 50 must be declared and may need to be returned.

## Disciplinary Process
Breaches of this code may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Employees have the right to be informed of allegations, to respond, and to be accompanied by a colleague in formal meetings.
