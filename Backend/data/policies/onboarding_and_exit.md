# Onboarding and Exit

> SAMPLE POLICY — replace with your organisation's official document.

## Probation
New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. During probation the notice period is 2 weeks on either side.

## First Week
On day one you'll receive your laptop, access credentials, and an onboarding buddy. Complete the mandatory trainings (Code of Conduct, Information Security, and Prevention of Sexual Harassment) within your first 14 days.

## Resignation and Notice Period
After probation, the notice period is 60 days. Submit your resignation through the HR portal. Notice may be shortened at the manager's discretion, subject to handover completion.

## Final Settlement
Final settlement, including salary for days worked and encashment of unused annual leave (up to 30 days), is processed within 45 days of your last working day. Relieving and experience letters are issued after all company assets are returned.

## Exit Interview
All departing employees are invited to a confidential exit interview with HR.
