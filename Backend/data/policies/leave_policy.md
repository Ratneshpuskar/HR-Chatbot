# Leave Policy

> SAMPLE POLICY — replace with your organisation's official document.

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.67 days per month. Part-time employees accrue leave pro rata to their contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried forward into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March or they lapse. Leave cannot be encashed except on separation, as described in the Onboarding and Exit policy.

## Requesting Leave
Submit leave requests through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for leave of up to 3 days, and at least 15 working days in advance for longer periods. Your reporting manager approves or declines requests within 2 working days.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry forward. For absences longer than 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be submitted directly to HR through the HR portal. Do not share medical documents with your manager or in chat tools.

## Parental Leave
- Birthing parents: 26 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Non-birthing parents: 4 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- Adoptive parents: 12 weeks of fully paid leave for the primary caregiver.

Notify HR at least 8 weeks before the expected start date where possible.

## Bereavement Leave
Up to 5 days of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse/partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or in-law), and 2 days for extended family.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published on the HR portal each December for the following year.
