# Payroll and Compensation

> SAMPLE POLICY — replace with your organisation's official document.

## Pay Schedule
Salaries are paid monthly on the last working day of the month. Payslips are available on the HR portal by the 1st of the following month.

## Payroll Queries
For questions about your payslip, deductions, or tax, raise a ticket in the HR portal under "Payroll". Do not email bank details or identity documents; update them only through the secure HR portal.

## Tax Declarations
Investment and tax-saving declarations must be submitted on the HR portal by 30 April for the financial year. Proofs must be uploaded by 31 January.

## Salary Reviews
Compensation is reviewed annually in April, based on performance ratings and market benchmarks. Mid-year adjustments are made only for promotions or exceptional circumstances.

## Expense Reimbursement
Submit business expenses through the expense portal within 30 days, with itemised receipts. Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll.
