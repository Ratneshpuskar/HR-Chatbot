# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

> SAMPLE POLICY — replace with your organisation's official document.

## Eligibility
Employees in roles designated as hybrid-eligible may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing their probation period. Your role designation is shown in your HR portal profile.

## Core Hours
All employees, regardless of location, must be available between 11:00 and 16:00 local office time for meetings and collaboration.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard and headset. A one-time home office setup allowance of USD 300 (or local equivalent) is available to hybrid employees and can be claimed through the expense portal within 3 months of becoming eligible.

## Working From Another Location
Working from a location other than your registered home address for more than 2 weeks, or from another country for any length of time, requires prior written approval from your manager and HR due to tax and legal implications.

## Information Security
When working remotely you must use the company VPN, lock your screen when away, and never use public or shared computers to access company systems.
