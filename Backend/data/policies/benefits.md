# Employee Benefits

## Health Insurance
All full-time employees and their dependants (spouse/partner and up to two children) are covered under the group medical insurance plan from day one of employment. Coverage includes hospitalisation up to the sum insured, day-care procedures, and maternity benefits.

Employees may add parents to the plan at their own cost during the annual enrolment window in January.

To file a claim, use the insurer's portal or app. Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals. For reimbursement claims, submit documents within 30 days of discharge directly to the insurer.

## Life and Accident Insurance
Term life cover of 3x annual fixed salary and personal accident cover of 3x annual fixed salary are provided at no cost to the employee.

## Retirement Benefits
The company contributes to the statutory retirement / provident fund as required by law. Gratuity is payable on completion of 5 years of continuous service, in line with applicable legislation.

## Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)
The EAP offers free, confidential counselling for employees and their families, 24/7, covering mental health, stress, relationships, financial and legal concerns. Contact details are on the HR portal under "Wellbeing". Usage is never reported to managers.

## Learning and Development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of USD 1,000 (or local equivalent) for courses, certifications, and conferences relevant to their role, subject to manager approval.

## Wellness Allowance
A monthly wellness allowance of USD 50 (or local equivalent) can be claimed for gym memberships, fitness classes, or wellness apps.
